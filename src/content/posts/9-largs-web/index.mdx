There's a new name in web design in Largs. Appropriately enough, it's [Largs Web](https://largsweb.com).

I've been building my own businesses and their websites for a while now, including Staycraft, Forcepull and Digitalic. Largs Web brings that experience to small businesses across Ayrshire and further afield.

The offer is straightforward: a professional website that shows customers what you do and makes it easy to get in touch. Prices start at £450, with the build price and any ongoing costs agreed before work starts. You'll work directly with me throughout.

We designed and built the site in less than a day, using AI coding tools and a lot of back and forth over the details. The miniature shopfront image made a huge difference. Then came the work showcase, the pricing, the contact form, and all the small adjustments that made it feel finished.

I'm really pleased with how it turned out. It's one of those projects where each round of changes made the whole thing better.
